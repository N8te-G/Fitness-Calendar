🏃 Fitness Calendar

Fit your Runna runs around your shifts

Connect your Google Calendar. The app reads your shifts, checks where Runna has put your runs, and moves them to days that fit these rules:

  • Easy runs only on 1st Late or 2nd Late days
  • Long runs on a day off, or a 2nd Early as a last resort
  • Speed sessions on any of those days
  • Always a rest day between runs

First, add your Google client ID in . The origin must be listed under “Authorised JavaScript origins” for that client.

Everything runs in your browser. Your calendar data goes only between this page and Google.
