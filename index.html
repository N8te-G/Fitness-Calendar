🏃 Fitness Calendar

Fit your Runna runs around your week

Connect Google Calendar to see your Runna plan next to work and everything else. The app shows which runs clash and suggests better times. It keeps hard sessions apart and long runs on the weekend, and you decide what to move.

One-time setup: add a Google OAuth client ID in . The README explains how to create one. Add as an authorised JavaScript origin.

Everything runs in your browser. Your calendar data is read directly from Google and isn't sent anywhere else.
